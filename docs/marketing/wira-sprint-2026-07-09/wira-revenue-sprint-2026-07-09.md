# Wira Revenue Sprint - July 9, 2026

## Sprint Position

Wira should not lead as "your first personal agent."

For this sprint, lead with the business pain:

**Missed follow-up is where small businesses leak money. Wira gives the owner an operator in WhatsApp so the next reply, draft, reminder, or handoff does not disappear.**

## Confidence Filter

- Buyer: small business owner / solo operator who already lives in WhatsApp.
- Painful event: customer asks, lead goes cold, owner forgets follow-up, staff needs a clear next reply.
- Concrete output: Wira drafts the next reply, summarizes context, prepares follow-up, and asks before risky sends.
- Low-risk first action: founder story post + workflow review + $49 local install or managed setup conversation.
- Trackable channel: LinkedIn founder post, Meta retargeting later, Ni Biashara audience, warm small-business outreach.
- Risk contained: owner-operated by default; no public auto-replies as the first promise.

Score: **8/10** if sold as owner follow-up leverage, **5/10** if sold as generic AI agent infrastructure.

## Offer Wedge

Do not sell "AI agent."

Sell:

**A WhatsApp operator for owners who lose money in the gap between "I saw the message" and "I actually followed up."**

First sprint offer:

- Founder-led workflow review.
- Show 3 missed follow-up patterns.
- Offer Wira Local for owners who want the agent on their own machine.
- Offer managed setup only if they clearly need business-number/Cloud API handling.

Wira exists for that gap.
